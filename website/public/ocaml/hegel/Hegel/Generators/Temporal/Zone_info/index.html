Module Temporal.Zone_info

What a zone source knows about a time zone name; see info.

type tag =
  1. | Fixed
    (*

    No transition after 1970.

    *)
  2. | Whole_hour
    (*

    Every offset after 1970 is a whole number of hours.

    *)
  3. | Fractional
    (*

    Some offset after 1970 is a whole number of minutes but not of hours.

    *)
  4. | Seconds
    (*

    Some offset in the zone's history is not a whole number of minutes.

    *)
  5. | Dst
    (*

    The current rule has a DST offset exactly one hour from standard.

    *)
  6. | Dst_other
    (*

    The current rule has a DST offset of another size.

    *)
  7. | Multi
    (*

    Some calendar year has more than two offset changes.

    *)
  8. | Base_change
    (*

    The lowest offset in force during some year after 1970 differs from the previous year's.

    *)
  9. | Midnight
    (*

    A transition after 1970 whose wall time before or after it is midnight.

    *)
  10. | Spans_midnight
    (*

    A transition after 1970 whose wall times before and after it fall on different dates.

    *)
  11. | Whole_day
    (*

    A transition that skips or repeats a whole day.

    *)
  12. | Same_offset
    (*

    A transition after 1970 that changes the abbreviation but not the offset.

    *)
  13. | Undefined
    (*

    The zone uses the -00 undefined-offset abbreviation.

    *)
  14. | Link
    (*

    The name is a link to another zone.

    *)
  15. | Legacy
    (*

    The name is not Area/Location for a current area.

    *)
  16. | Inverted_sign
    (*

    The name is one of the Etc/GMT+5 family.

    *)

A feature of a time zone, derived from its offsets and name. "After 1970" means at any instant from 1970-01-01T00:00:00Z on, the rule in force after the last explicit transition included.

type t = {
  1. primary_name : string;
  2. is_link : bool;
  3. rank : int;
  4. tags : tag list;
}

primary_name is the resolved zone name (Asia/Kolkata for the link Asia/Calcutta). rank is its position in the shrinking order (UTC is 0). tags lists its features in declaration order.
