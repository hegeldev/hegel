Module-level defaults

These modules declare @@ portable as their default: Hegel, Hegel.Generators, Hegel.Derive, Hegel.Settings, Hegel_jane, Hegel_jane_concurrent, and the Hegel.Stateful submodules Hegel.Stateful.Pool, Hegel.Stateful.Rule, Hegel.Stateful.Invariant, and Hegel.Stateful.Concurrent_rule. The Hegel.Stateful runners (Hegel.Stateful.run, Hegel.Stateful.run_concurrent) are not portable.
